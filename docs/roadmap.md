# Roadmap (parked ideas)

Brainstormed but deliberately NOT built yet (in rough impact order). Kept here so future sessions don't re-derive them:

1. **"What should we watch tonight?" decider** — a button that pulls from the group's combined watchlists and either shuffles a pick or runs a quick group vote. (Real-time/synchronous watch-parties are explicitly OUT of scope.)
2. **Smart recommendations from group taste** — surface "3 people loved this and you haven't seen it" / "you both saved Dune" using existing ratings + watchlist data.
3. **Year-in-review / "Wrapped"** — per-person and per-group recap (total logged, top genre, highest-rated, harshest critic, most-watched month).
4. **Taste-compatibility score** — "you and Sarah agree 82% on thrillers," computed from shared ratings.
5. **Episode/season progress for TV** — track where each member is (S2E4) and "who's caught up / no spoilers past here," turning a show from one entry into ongoing progress.

Also deferred: **email/push delivery of the weekly digest** (the in-app digest card is built; delivery needs an email integration + scheduler).
